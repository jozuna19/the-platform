The Platform · Design Recon

The Design Playbook

What the best fitness apps actually do — MacroFactor, Hevy, MyFitnessPal, Cal AI, Cronometer, Whoop & Oura — distilled into patterns worth stealing and a ranked plan for The Platform.

Nobody mainstream combines macro tracking + a lifting log + a real conversational AI coach in one app. That's The Platform's whole edge. The job to win: log in seconds, know where I stand at a glance, stay accountable — one-handed, on a cut.

What each app teaches us

One clear lesson from each, mapped to the tab it informs.

MacroFactor

Food · Coach · Dashboard

  • One-tap log from anywhere — a persistent scan/search strip above the tab bar.
  • Consumed ↔ remaining toggle on the hero macro widget.
  • Trend weight (smoothed line) beats raw scale dots — cheap math, huge calm.
  • Weekly check-in as a conversational accept/decline target change.

Hevy

Train

  • Pre-fill last session + 1-tap complete — unchanged set = a single checkmark.
  • Set row = # · prev · wt · reps · ✓, aligned numerics.
  • 3 row states (pending → active → green done); checkmark auto-starts rest timer.
  • Steal Strong's inline PR flash the instant a set beats your best.

MyFitnessPal + Cal AI

Food

  • Recent / frequent foods first, inline + to one-tap add.
  • MFP's 2026 redesign cratered to 1.5★ for adding taps — keep it flat.
  • Cal AI's viral trick: meal photos as the log feed (thumbnails, alive).
  • Keep a Quick Add (calories/macros only) escape hatch.

Cronometer

Body · Dashboard

  • One bar primitive (name · bar · amount · %) rendered many times.
  • ≤3 status colors, unambiguous: grey = under, green = in range, red = over max.
  • Curated by default, expand for all — their overwhelm is the warning.

Whoop + Oura

Coach · Digest · Body

  • Coach reply = metric+number → cause → one action.
  • One hero number + colored ring + one-line verdict; 3-color bands reused everywhere.
  • Compare to your own baseline, not absolutes ("vs your 3-week avg").
  • Oura's near-free premium tell: one phrase per heading in italic serif.

Carbon Diet Coach

Weekly Digest

  • 3-section recap: Updated Targets · Prescribed vs Actual · Goal Progress.
  • Coach voice is restrained, not hype — it explains a no-change too.
  • Targets always shown as net change + reason, never a bare number.

The build plan for The Platform

Ranked by impact ÷ effort. Tags: quick medium bigger

  1. Bottom tab bar + center "+" FAB quick

    Move nav to the bottom thumb zone (Home · Food · Train · Body) with a raised center + that opens a quick-log bottom sheet. Every mainstream app agrees; it's the single biggest "native app" upgrade and puts your #1 action dead-center.

  2. Food tab: one hero number + recents-first logging medium

    Make calories remaining the huge hero (72–120px, condensed face), macros as thin bars/rings under it with a consumed↔remaining toggle. Surface Recent/Frequent foods with inline + before search or AI. Log path: FAB → bottom sheet → recents → optimistic instant ring update.

  3. Train tab: the Hevy set-row + auto rest timer medium

    Pre-fill last session's weight×reps as ghost values so an unchanged set is one checkmark. Row states pending→active→green. Checkmark auto-fires a circular rest timer (±15s). Add an inline PR flash when a set beats your est-1RM best.

  4. Trend weight line quick

    Replace/overlay raw scale dots with a smoothed trend line (moving average) on the Body tab. Pure front-end math, and it's the most-praised feature in the whole category — kills weigh-in anxiety on a cut.

  5. A real Home/Dashboard as the landing screen bigger

    Open on a glance-dashboard, not Train: hero calories-remaining ring + protein, today's workout status, trend-weight sparkline, streak. Progressive disclosure — overview first, drill in on tap. One thing dominates.

  6. Coach upgrades: chips, data-cards-in-chat, tone toggle medium

    Seed the empty chat with suggested-prompt chips; render real macro/weight cards inline in replies; enforce the metric→cause→action reply shape; add an Oura-style tone (Encouraging/Direct) toggle. Ground every number in a real data row.

  7. Weekly Digest → Carbon's 3-section shape quick

    Restructure the digest into Updated Targets (net change + why, incl. "stay the course") · Prescribed vs Actual · Goal Progress, compared to your own 3-week baseline. You already have the endpoint — this is mostly prompt + layout.

Do / Don't

✓ Do

  • One hero number per screen, huge, condensed face, tabular-nums.
  • ONE accent, used only for progress + CTAs. Near-black base, not pure #000.
  • Three fixed macro colors, identical everywhere (rings, bars, legend).
  • Recents-first logging; optimistic instant updates; bottom sheets over full modals.
  • Compare to the user's own baseline. Explain a no-change.
  • Restrained motion: spring 150–250ms, count-ups, one celebration for real wins.

✕ Don't

  • Trade daily-driver speed for prettier cards (MFP fell to 1.5★ doing exactly this).
  • Add taps or bury per-meal calories behind navigation.
  • Dump all data at once (Cronometer's overwhelm) — curate, expand on demand.
  • Glow everything, rainbow gradients, buzz-on-every-tap — the AI-slop tells.
  • Let the coach invent numbers; anchor to a real row.
  • Hard-block logging on incomplete fields, or use scroll-dials for dates.

Layout rules

RuleSpec
NavBottom bar, 4 tabs + center FAB. Tap targets ≥48px. Scroll padding-bottom ~72px.
BaseNear-black #0B0B0C, surfaces by lightening not borders; text via white-opacity.
Rings vs barsRings for the 1–2 hero goals; thin bars for the 4-macro breakdown. Low-opacity unfilled track on dark.
MotionSpring 150–250ms; count-up on stats via requestAnimationFrame; conic-gradient rings, no library.
FeedbackVibration API light 10ms tick on log-confirm; optimistic UI everywhere.
